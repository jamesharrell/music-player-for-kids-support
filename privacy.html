Music Player for Kids Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026.

Music Player for Kids is designed for a young child to play music chosen by a grown-up. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, social features, or external purchase paths.

What the app stores

The app stores the kids playlists a grown-up creates or imports, approved song identifiers, playlist names, playlist colors, theme settings, playback preferences, and app settings. This information is used only to run the kids music experience.

Apple Music and purchases

Apple Music permission, playback, catalog search, and subscription purchase processing are handled by Apple. Music Player for Kids receives only the information needed to play approved songs and check whether Premium themes are unlocked.

iCloud sharing

If a grown-up enables iCloud or family sharing for this app, kids playlist data can sync through the signed-in Apple ID and can be shared with invited family members through Apple services.

Children's data

The app does not ask children for names, email addresses, birth dates, location, photos, messages, or contacts. It does not send child data to third-party advertising or analytics services.

Deleting data

A grown-up can delete kids playlists inside the app. Deleting the app removes local app data from the device. iCloud-synced or shared data can also be managed through the Apple ID and iCloud settings used for the app.
